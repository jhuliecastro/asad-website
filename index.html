Who We Are

Built on Trust,
Driven by Quality

Al Shurooq Al Dhabi HVAC Systems LLC is one of the Gulf's leading HVAC specialists — manufacturing, supplying, and installing premium air conditioning systems since 2015 from our headquarters in Deira, Dubai.

Our mission is simple: Only Customer Satisfaction — through quality equipment, professional installation, and genuine after-sales care. We've delivered solutions for government agencies, commercial towers, hotels, and luxury villas across all seven emirates.

What We Do

Complete HVAC
Solutions

Every service, every system — we cover the full lifecycle of your climate control needs.

❄️

AC Installation & Supply

Full supply and installation of ducted splits, packaged units, chillers, fan coil units, air handling units, and VRF systems.

🌀

Ductwork & Air Balancing

Professional duct fabrication, chilled water systems, air balancing, and VCD/VAV equipment maintenance.

🔧

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled AC maintenance programs — FCU, AHU, humidifier and dehumidifier servicing — all year round.

⚙️

Spare Parts Supply

Genuine parts: compressors, motors, copper coils, condensers, expansion valves, control boards, and blowers.

📡

VRF Systems

Design, supply, and installation of Variable Refrigerant Flow systems — the most energy-efficient solution for large buildings.

🏗️

Turnkey Projects

Full project execution from engineering design through commissioning, handover, and annual maintenance contracts.
